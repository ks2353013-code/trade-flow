# TradeFlow Email Sequences

## Sequence A — New lead
Subject: Your export workflow, in one workspace

Hi,

TradeFlow is an AI operating system for global trade. It connects trade research, buyer and supplier discovery, verification, CRM, AI-assisted outreach and operational workflows in one workspace.

The easiest way to understand it is to run a trade mission around one product and one market.

See TradeFlow in action.

## Sequence B — Education
Subject: A buyer list is not a sales pipeline

Finding companies is only the first step.

The real workflow is:
Discovery → Verification → CRM → Outreach → Follow-up → Operation.

TradeFlow connects those stages so the context does not disappear between tools.

— TradeFlow

## Sequence C — Product
Subject: Meet the AI export team

TradeFlow includes AI workflows for research, buyer discovery, supplier discovery, CRM, compliance, revenue intelligence and outreach.

The goal is not to replace your team. It is to give your team an intelligent operating layer.

— TradeFlow

## Sequence D — Conversion
Subject: Run the TradeFlow Export Challenge

## Sequence E — Pilot-safe
Subject: AI-assisted, human-controlled

TradeFlow keeps important commercial actions inside an approval workflow.

During the controlled pilot, email remains Dry Run and autonomous execution, schedulers, WhatsApp and calls are disabled.

The product experience focuses on intelligence, discovery, verification, CRM and AI-assisted workflow.

— TradeFlow
