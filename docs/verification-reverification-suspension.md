# Reverification and suspension process

Every approval has a bounded validity date. A verified company that starts a material update creates a new version, loses the active badge immediately, and returns to Draft. It must resubmit for manual review.

TradeFlow may suspend an active verification when evidence becomes unreliable, identifiers conflict, material company details change, or policy requires investigation. Master Admin confirmation and a reason are mandatory. Suspension immediately removes the public badge.

Expiry/revocation also requires Master Admin confirmation and a recorded reason. Expiring-soon and expired notifications are internal while email remains in Dry Run. Reverification follows the current criteria and document requirements; an old approval does not automatically carry forward.
