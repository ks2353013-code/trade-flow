# TradeFlow × UPITS 2026 Acquisition Campaign

## Why this event

UP International Trade Show 2026 is scheduled for 25–29 September 2026 at India Expo Centre & Mart, Greater Noida. Official event material describes a large exporter, manufacturer and buyer audience across sectors including MSME, ODOP, agro, textiles, electronics/IT, auto/EV, pharma/healthcare, packaging, leather/footwear and logistics.

This overlaps directly with TradeFlow's initial Indian-exporter ICP.

## Campaign

### Name
**TradeFlow Export Opportunity Desk**

### Core message

> **Going to UPITS? Don't just collect buyer cards. Know where your product has the strongest market evidence first.**

### Offer

Free Export Challenge:
- product
- target market
- legitimate HS-6 selection
- real trade data where available
- competitor supplier context
- tariff information where available
- evidence/provenance

Then:
> **Turn the result into a TradeFlow mission.**

Which company fits?

Who follows up?
