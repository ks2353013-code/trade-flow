# TradeFlow Competitive Recon

## Core product loop

TradeFlow should win the complete global-trade operating loop:

Discover -> Verify -> Qualify -> Research -> CRM -> Outreach -> Negotiate -> Convert -> Analyze -> Automate

The differentiator is not another directory or generic CRM. It is an AI operating layer connecting these stages.

## Benchmark map

| Benchmark | Primary job | TradeFlow learning |
|---|---|---|
| IndiaMART | B2B discovery and lead generation | Lead quality, buyer/supplier intent and verification must be stronger than a raw marketplace lead |
| Alibaba.com | Global sourcing + transaction protection | Trust signals, RFQ, supplier assessment and transaction confidence matter |
| Global Sources | International supplier discovery | Verification must translate into actionable confidence, not merely a badge |
| Apollo | Sales intelligence + outreach | Contact intelligence, enrichment and outreach should feel unified |
| HubSpot | CRM + automation + AI | CRM should be operational, automated and measurable rather than a passive database |

## Hard parts

- Multi-tenant isolation
- AI-agent orchestration
- Lead verification evidence
- Source freshness and confidence
- Email deliverability and approval controls
- Subscription/entitlement enforcement
- Real-time collaboration
- Cross-company permissions
- Auditability of AI actions
- Reliable analytics from operational events
