# TradeFlow Customer Acquisition Engine

## Objective

Turn TradeFlow from a product people can discover into a product that repeatedly creates qualified exporter conversations, product trials, missions, and paid conversions.

This system is designed for an early-stage product with no assumed customer count, no fabricated case studies, and a controlled pilot where email remains Dry Run.

## Core commercial thesis

Do not sell "AI software" first.

Sell the outcome:

> **Give TradeFlow your product and target market. See the trade opportunity, the competitive landscape, and the companies worth investigating. Then turn that research into a working sales mission.**

The acquisition wedge is the **TradeFlow Export Challenge**.

## ICP

### Primary
1. Indian MSME manufacturers already exporting.
2. Merchant exporters with a repeatable product catalogue.
3. Export sales teams operating from spreadsheets, email and messaging apps.
4. Manufacturers trying to enter a second or third foreign market.
5. Export consultants/agencies who manage research for multiple clients.

### Initial verticals

Prioritize categories where product classification and trade data are reasonably structured:
- engineering components
- textiles and garments
- processed food/agri products
- handicrafts/home products
- chemicals/materials
- packaging
- electronics/electrical products
- leather/footwear

Avoid broad "all exporters" messaging. Every campaign should name a product category and market.

## Content engine

Every week publish:

### 3 data-led posts
Example:
> "We tested [product] in [market]. Here is what the actual trade data says."

### 2 product demos
- product → market → trade intelligence
- buyer → verification → CRM

### 1 founder/operator post
Talk about the problem of disconnected export workflows.

### 1 direct CTA
> "Give us one product and one market. We will show you what TradeFlow can uncover."

## Short-form video formula

0–2 sec: painful question  
2–7 sec: product + market input  
7–15 sec: real result  
15–23 sec: buyer/verification workflow  
23–30 sec: CTA

Never manufacture a buyer, market statistic, revenue result, customer count or success story.

## Outbound motion

Use account-based outreach rather than bulk spam.

### Prospect trigger
- exporter with a visible product catalogue
- existing export markets
- new-market expansion language
- participation in a trade fair
- export hiring
- international buyer activity
- product category matching a TradeFlow demo

### First message

> Hi [Name] — I came across [Company] and saw that you work with [product/category].
>
> We are building TradeFlow, an AI operating system for exporters.
>
> Instead of sending you a generic demo, I can run the Export Challenge for your product and one target market using source-backed trade data.
>
> If the result is useful, I can show you the workflow from market research → buyer discovery → verification → CRM.
>
> No pitch deck required.

### Follow-up 1

> I can run the analysis around one specific market you are considering. If you tell me the market, I will use that as the example.

### Follow-up 2

> The useful part is not a buyer list. It is connecting market evidence, company evidence and the sales workflow so the research becomes actionable.

### Follow-up 3

> Closing the loop here. If international-market research is on your roadmap, TradeFlow's Export Challenge is the easiest way to see the product.

Do not send unsolicited bulk messages or scrape private contact information. Personalize legitimate business outreach and respect opt-outs.

- Export Promotion Councils
- India SME Forum
- sector associations
- trade-fair communities
- LinkedIn exporter communities

2. answer a real exporter question
3. offer a free Export Challenge
4. move interested users to TradeFlow

Potential referral reward can be defined later; do not promise discounts until implemented.

## Non-negotiables

- No fabricated metrics.
- No fake customers.
- No fake testimonials.
- No buyer guarantees.
- No "verified" label without evidence.
- No claim of autonomous live outreach while the pilot is Dry Run.
- No mass spam.
- No pretending a click is a customer.
- Optimize for activated exporters and paid workspaces, not vanity reach.

## Success definition

The marketing system is working when an exporter can enter through a piece of content, complete the Export Challenge, understand the evidence, create a mission, see a qualified opportunity and naturally reach the paid workspace without requiring a sales presentation.
