# Verification badge meaning and disclaimer

The backend publishes a badge only while a verification is `Verified` and its validity date is in the future. Draft, Submitted, Under Review, More Information Required, Rejected, Suspended, and Expired records have no active public badge.

Available labels are TradeFlow Verified Exporter, Importer, Exporter & Importer, Trading Company, Manufacturer, and Supplier. The public response includes the legal/trade name, category, country, review and validity dates, reference ID, status, and evidence level. It excludes PAN, GSTIN, IEC, personal information, and documents.

Required disclaimer:

> TradeFlow verification confirms that specified business information and supporting documents were reviewed at the stated date. It is not a guarantee of product quality, payment, delivery, legal compliance, or future performance.

The badge is TradeFlow’s review statement, not a government certification.
