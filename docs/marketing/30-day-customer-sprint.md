# TradeFlow 30-Day Customer Sprint

## Goal

Create the first repeatable acquisition engine around the Export Challenge.

This is an execution schedule, not a vanity content calendar.

## Days 1–3 — Positioning

Publish:
1. "Don't guess where to export."
2. "A buyer list is not a sales pipeline."
3. "Give me one product and one country."

Every post links to the Export Challenge.

## Days 4–7 — Product proof

Publish:
- one live trade-data walkthrough
- one HS-6 classification walkthrough
- one buyer verification walkthrough
- one mission → CRM walkthrough

Create one 30-second product video from the strongest workflow.

## Days 8–10 — Exporter conversations

Target:
- Indian manufacturers
- merchant exporters
- export managers
- companies announcing overseas expansion
- companies exhibiting at upcoming trade fairs

Offer a personalized Export Challenge rather than a generic demo.

## Days 11–14 — Market-specific campaign

Choose three product categories.

For each:
- one target market
- one data-led post
- one short video
- one challenge example
- one CTA

The winning category is the one producing the strongest qualified conversations and activation, not the most views.

## Days 15–18 — UPITS campaign

UPITS 2026 runs 25–29 September in Greater Noida.

Create:
- pre-event post
- "prepare your market before the show" post
- live event workflow demo
- post-event "turn conversations into CRM" post

Target UP/Delhi-NCR exporters and relevant sector communities.

## Days 19–21 — Founder-led conversion

Publish:
> "I built TradeFlow because export research should not end in a spreadsheet."

Then show the actual workflow.

Offer limited founder-assisted onboarding during the controlled pilot.

## Days 22–24 — Objection handling

Content:
- "Is TradeFlow just a buyer database?"
- "Where do the numbers come from?"
- "How does verification work?"
- "Can my team use it together?"
- "Does AI send emails automatically?"

Answer honestly:
- TradeFlow is a connected operating workflow.
- Data has provenance.
- verification is evidence-gated.
- teams have workspaces/roles.
- pilot email is Dry Run and commercial actions remain human-controlled.

## Days 25–27 — Conversion push

Retarget:
- challenge users
- pricing visitors
- signup abandoners
- engaged social viewers

Message:

> **You have the market research. Now turn it into a mission.**

## Days 28–30 — Measure and narrow

Rank channels by:
1. activated exporters
2. qualified conversations
3. paid conversions
4. cost per activated exporter

Do not rank by followers or impressions alone.

Double down on the strongest two acquisition motions.

## First customer conversation structure

1. What product do you export?
2. Which markets do you currently sell into?
3. Which market are you trying to enter?
4. How do you currently find buyers?
5. Where does your pipeline live?
6. What happens after a buyer is found?
7. Run the TradeFlow Challenge.
8. Show the mission.
9. Show verification.
10. Show CRM.
11. Show outreach approval.
12. Offer the appropriate workspace.

The demo should follow the customer's workflow rather than a feature checklist.

## Customer qualification

A prospect is high-intent when at least two are true:
- actively exports
- has a specific product
- has a target market
- has a sales team
- has a current buyer-acquisition problem
- has a current export pipeline
- is already spending time/money on trade research

## Offer

### Founder-assisted pilot

> "Bring one product and one target market. We will help you turn the opportunity into a working TradeFlow mission."

The pilot should emphasize real workflows and evidence, not free consulting forever.

## Conversion language

Avoid:
- "revolutionary"
- "guaranteed buyers"
- "10x exports"
- "AI will close deals"
- unsupported customer numbers

Use:
- source-backed
- evidence-first
- workflow-connected
- human-controlled
- real trade data
- verified where evidence supports verification

## Target outcome

By the end of the sprint, the system should produce measurable data on:
- which product categories respond
- which markets generate interest
- which acquisition channel creates activated users
- which objections block conversion
- which plan users naturally select

The sprint is successful only when it creates qualified product usage and revenue signals.
