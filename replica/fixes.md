# TradeFlow Competitive Fix Plan

## P0 — Trust layer

### 1. Verification Evidence Card
Show:
- legal/business identity evidence
- source URLs
- verification timestamp
- source freshness
- confidence score
- contradictions
- unresolved checks

Why: competitors commonly expose badges or profiles; TradeFlow should explain *why* a lead is trusted.

### 2. Lead Freshness Engine
Every lead/contact should carry:
- last verified date
- source age
- email confidence
- company activity signals
- stale-data warning

Why: Apollo feedback specifically highlights outdated contact information. citeturn1search11

## P1 — Commercial intelligence

### 3. Trade Intent Score
Combine:
- product/category fit
- geography
- buying/selling intent
- volume indicators
- company fit
- contact seniority
- freshness
- verification confidence

Output: one explainable score plus evidence.

### 4. AI Research Timeline
Every important CRM record gets an evidence timeline:
research -> verification -> outreach -> response -> negotiation -> conversion.

## P1 — Automation

### 5. Mission-to-Revenue workflow
A mission should be able to:
discover -> verify -> qualify -> propose outreach -> request approval -> send -> track -> update CRM -> recommend next action.

External actions remain approval-gated unless explicitly configured otherwise.

### 6. Smart Outreach Guardrails
Before sending:
- duplicate check
- tenant check
- approval status
- recipient validity
- unsubscribe/compliance check
- rate limit
- audit event

## P2 — Differentiation

### 7. Trade Negotiation Desk
Turn email/WhatsApp conversations and commercial terms into structured:
- quotation
- MOQ
- Incoterm
- payment terms
- lead time
- sample status
- objections
- next action

### 8. Global Trade Risk View
Combine supplier/company verification, compliance findings and transaction signals into a decision-ready risk panel.

## Positioning direction

TradeFlow should position around:

**From finding a trade lead to actually working the opportunity.**

The product advantage is the connected operating system, not one isolated discovery feature.
