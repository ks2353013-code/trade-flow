# Verification criteria matrix

| Category | Core evidence | Additional evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exporter | Registration, PAN, address, representative authority | IEC, product and market declarations | Does not imply importer or manufacturer |
| Importer | Registration, PAN, address, representative authority | IEC, product and market declarations | Does not imply exporter |
| Exporter & Importer | Registration, PAN, address, representative authority | IEC, product and market declarations | Both activities must be supported |
| Trading Company | Registration, PAN, address, representative authority | Business activity declaration; relationships optional | Does not imply manufacturer |
| Manufacturer | Registration, PAN, address, representative authority | Factory/operating address and production declaration; licences when applicable | Product-specific licences are not inferred |
| Supplier | Registration, PAN, address, representative authority | Supporting commercial evidence when needed | Does not verify individual leads |

GST evidence is required where applicable. Identifiers receive structural validation, not official confirmation. Missing/expired evidence blocks approval. Duplicate identifiers across unrelated companies create a blocking risk flag.
