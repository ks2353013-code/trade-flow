# TradeFlow Positioning

## Category
AI Operating System for Global Trade

## One-line
TradeFlow helps exporters discover opportunities, verify businesses, manage relationships, automate trade workflows and run their export operation from one intelligent workspace.

## Primary promise
**Find opportunities. Make the deal. Run the operation.**

## Supporting promise
From market research and buyer/supplier discovery to verification, CRM, outreach, compliance, documents and team workflows.

## Short pitch
TradeFlow is the AI operating system for modern exporters. It connects trade intelligence, buyer and supplier discovery, verification, CRM, AI-assisted outreach, compliance, documents and team operations in one workspace.

## Product story
1. Intelligence — understand markets, companies and opportunities.
2. Discovery — identify buyers and suppliers.
3. Verification — turn raw companies into qualified opportunities.
4. Deal — manage CRM, outreach, negotiation and revenue.
5. Operation — coordinate compliance, documents, tasks and people.

## AI team framing
- Research Agent — turns a trade question into structured intelligence.
- Buyer Discovery Agent — finds potential buyer opportunities.
- Supplier Discovery Agent — finds supplier opportunities.
- CRM Agent — keeps commercial work organized.
- Compliance Agent — supports trade-compliance workflows.
- Revenue Agent — surfaces pipeline and revenue intelligence.
- Outreach Agent — drafts personalized outreach for human review.

## Positioning hierarchy
Primary: AI Operating System for Global Trade
Secondary: Your AI export team in one workspace
Entry point: Discover and verify better trade opportunities
Proof: Mission → verified lead → CRM → approved outreach → operational workflow

## Audience messages
### New/expanding exporter
“Turn export research into a repeatable workflow.”

### Established exporter
“Stop running international sales across spreadsheets, inboxes and disconnected tools.”

### Export team
“Give every person one workspace with role-based access, CRM, missions and AI assistance.”

## Messaging rules
- Sell the workflow, not the feature count.
- Show product behavior rather than abstract AI claims.
- Never claim live automation that is disabled in the controlled pilot.
- Keep “AI” tied to a concrete job: research, discovery, verification, CRM, outreach, compliance or revenue.
