# TradeFlow Export Challenge

## Campaign name
TRADEFLOW EXPORT CHALLENGE

## Core hook
**Give TradeFlow your product. Give it your target market. See what it can uncover.**

## Experience
Input:
- Product
- Target country/market
- Buyer type
- Optional company context

Output story:
1. Market research
2. Buyer discovery
3. Company intelligence
4. Verification signals
5. Opportunity record
6. CRM handoff
7. Outreach draft
8. Recommended next actions

## 30-second video script
0–3s: “What if you could turn one export idea into a structured sales mission?”
3–7s: Enter product and country.
7–14s: Show research and buyer discovery.
14–20s: Show verification and opportunity record.
20–26s: Push to CRM and generate outreach draft.
26–30s: “TradeFlow — the AI Operating System for Global Trade.”

## Landing CTA
**Run the Export Challenge**

## Social hook variants
- “Give us one product and one country.”
- “Let's see what an AI trade mission looks like.”
- “From product → market → buyer → CRM.”
- “Stop researching in ten tabs.”
- “Your export workflow, rebuilt around AI.”

## Safety/accuracy
Use real product output in demonstrations. Do not invent buyer identities, verification results, transaction volume, revenue or customer success metrics.
