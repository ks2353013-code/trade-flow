# TradeFlow Marketing Launch System

TradeFlow is positioned as an AI Operating System for Global Trade.

Core commercial story:
**Find the opportunity → make the deal → run the operation.**

Primary audience:
- Indian MSME exporters
- Merchant exporters
- Export sales teams
- Export-led companies

Product proof used in this package:
- AI Command Center
- Research, Buyer Discovery, Supplier Discovery, CRM, Compliance, Revenue and Outreach agents
- Mission Center and Mission Intelligence
- Buyer/Supplier source connectors
- Lead Verification Engine
- CRM pipeline and revenue tracking
- AI outreach with approval queue
- Documents/PDF export
- Employee roles and permissions
- Multi-company/workspace architecture
- Notifications, analytics, tasks and support
- Subscription/entitlement controls

Pilot-safe claims:
- Email is Dry Run during the controlled pilot.
- WhatsApp, calls, schedulers and autonomous execution are disabled in the controlled pilot.
- Do not claim autonomous sending or live WhatsApp/call automation until those capabilities are actually enabled.

Launch campaign:
**TRADEFLOW EXPORT CHALLENGE**
“Give TradeFlow your product. Give it your target market. See what it can uncover.”

This directory contains the complete launch messaging, content, ad copy, email sequences, demo script, landing-page copy and campaign operating system.
