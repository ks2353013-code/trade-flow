# Business Verification user guide

Business Verification is available after company onboarding from the TradeFlow dashboard. It verifies a company, not a buyer, supplier lead, transaction, product, or future performance.

Company Owners and authorized Admins can save incomplete drafts, upload private supporting evidence, submit, respond to a request for more information, and start a new version after rejection or after material details change. Submitted and reviewed versions are locked.

Choose the claim that matches the real business activity. Exporter and Importer claims require IEC evidence. Trading Company verification does not imply manufacturing. Manufacturer verification requires operating/factory evidence.

Uploads accept PDF, PNG, and JPEG files up to 8 MB. Do not upload passwords, OTPs, payment-card details, bank credentials, Aadhaar, or unrelated personal identity documents.

Statuses are: Not Started, Draft, Submitted, Under Review, More Information Required, Verified, Rejected, Suspended, and Expired. Review time varies; TradeFlow does not guarantee a deadline or approval.

TradeFlow verification confirms that specified business information and supporting documents were reviewed at the stated date. It is not a guarantee of product quality, payment, delivery, legal compliance, or future performance.
