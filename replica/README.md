# TradeFlow Replica Intelligence

This directory adapts the open-source Replica methodology for TradeFlow.

## Purpose

Use competitor products as research subjects, not as source-code or asset-copy targets.

Primary questions:
- What jobs do competing products solve?
- Where are their workflows strong?
- What do customers repeatedly complain about?
- What capabilities are missing?
- Where can TradeFlow be materially better?
- Which gaps should become product, UX, automation, trust, or monetization priorities?

## Current competitor set

1. IndiaMART — India-first B2B marketplace and lead-generation benchmark.
2. Alibaba.com — global B2B sourcing, supplier verification, RFQ and transaction-protection benchmark.
3. Global Sources — international sourcing marketplace benchmark.
4. Apollo — sales intelligence, contact data and outreach benchmark.
5. HubSpot — CRM, marketing, sales automation and AI benchmark.

## Adapted workflow

recon -> architecture fit -> design/gap analysis -> build priorities -> test -> parity/gap score -> entrepreneur/user-complaint analysis -> roadmap

Unlike a literal app clone, TradeFlow keeps its own product identity, data model, brand, integrations and workflows.

## Guardrails

- Public sources and the user's own authorized accounts only.
- No private APIs, source-code extraction, credential collection, paywall bypassing, or scraping against terms.
- Rebuild ideas and functionality independently.
- Do not copy competitor branding, logos, proprietary copy, customer data, or protected assets.
- Treat review evidence as research, not testimonials.
