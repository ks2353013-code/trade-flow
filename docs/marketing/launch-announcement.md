# TradeFlow Launch Announcement

## Headline
**TradeFlow is building the AI operating system for global trade.**

## Body
Export businesses are expected to research markets, discover buyers, verify companies, manage pipelines, write outreach, coordinate compliance and keep teams aligned—often across disconnected tools.

TradeFlow brings that workflow into one intelligent workspace.

Its operating model connects:
- Trade research
- Buyer and supplier discovery
- Verification
- Mission intelligence
- CRM
- AI-assisted outreach
- Compliance workflows
- Documents
- Tasks
- Analytics
- Employees and permissions
- Multi-company workspaces

The product is built around a simple flow:

**Find the opportunity → make the deal → run the operation.**

TradeFlow's Export Challenge puts that idea into a simple experience: give TradeFlow a product and a target market, then explore the resulting trade mission.

TradeFlow is designed for modern exporters and export teams that want an intelligent operating layer around their international business.

**TradeFlow — AI Operating System for Global Trade.**
